Pizza or sushi on Friday? Beach or mountains? Ask, and let them vote. 🔴🔵

🔗 PRIVATE POLLS
Create the question, send it on WhatsApp, and only people with the link can vote. You'll see who voted what.

🌍 PUBLIC POLLS
Post a question for everyone and vote on other people's. For a week they show up on the home screen, by language. Only percentages, never who voted.

🔴 RED OR 🔵 BLUE
Two answers, half the screen each. Tap yours and that's it: the vote is final.

🔮 WHAT WILL MOST PEOPLE PICK?
After voting, guess which side wins. When the poll closes you'll know if you read the majority right.

⏳ YOU DECIDE HOW LONG
1 day, 7 days or no limit. Close it whenever you want.

🛡️ A FRIENDLY PLACE
Public polls go through a word filter, can be reported, and are hidden when several people report them.

🚫 NO ADS
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Just your name, your polls and your votes, and you can delete your data from the app.
