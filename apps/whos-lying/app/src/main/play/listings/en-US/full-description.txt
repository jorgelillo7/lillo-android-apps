The classic impostor word game for groups, on one phone. No ads, no subscriptions, every pack free. 🕵️

🤫 HOW TO PLAY
Pass the phone around and everyone sees their secret word. You all have the same one… except the impostor. Take turns giving clues, discuss, and vote someone out. Who's lying?

🎭 THREE MODES
• Classic: the impostor gets a similar word and doesn't even know they're the impostor.
• Blind: the impostor gets no word and has to bluff.
• The Drifter: plus a player with no word who wins by guessing it when voted out.

🌀 CHAOS
Turn on Chaos and some games get a random number of impostors… sometimes everyone.

📦 21 FREE PACKS, 600+ WORDS
Food, movies & TV, football, famous people, characters, music, brands, fantasy, summer, celebrations and more. Pick as many as you like, and no word comes back until you've played almost all of them. And make your own in seconds: one word per line.

⏱️ DISCUSSION TIMER
An optional countdown before each vote, so nobody drags it out.

🎨 DRAWING MODE AND SCORES
Instead of talking, draw the word together on one canvas and share the masterpiece at the end. Points add up game after game: who is the best liar in your group?

🚫 NO CATCH
No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet. Nothing leaves your phone.
