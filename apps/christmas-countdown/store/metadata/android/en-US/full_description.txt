How long until Christmas? Find out at a glance, down to the second. 🎄

⏱️ LIVE COUNTDOWN
Days, hours, minutes and seconds until Christmas Day, always counting towards the next Christmas. It never expires: on 26 December it starts counting again.

🌙 SLEEPS MODE
Switch to "sleeps" and count the nights left, just like kids do. In the last two weeks, a little moon appears for every night to go.

🎁 ADVENT CALENDAR
24 doors to open, one for each day of December until Christmas Eve. Behind every door there is a family activity (getting the house ready first, then things to do together, then getting together) or a Christmas song to play. They change every year, and you can share each day's surprise with your friends.

🏠 HOME-SCREEN WIDGET
Add the widget and see the days (or sleeps) left without opening the app.

🎶 CHRISTMAS CAROLS
Tap the music note to play five classic carols in random order, played by a music box: Silent Night, Jingle Bells, O Christmas Tree, Joy to the World and We Wish You a Merry Christmas.

❄️ AND MORE
• Gently falling snow
• Share the countdown with family and friends
• English and Spanish

🚫 NO ADS, NO TRACKING
The app does not collect any data and does not need an internet connection.
