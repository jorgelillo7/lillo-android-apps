Card tournament on Friday? Office ping-pong league? Set it up in seconds and see who's the last one standing. 👑

⚔️ KNOCKOUT OR SWISS
Knockout for any number of players (byes are spread for you) or Swiss, where everyone plays every round, with a final knockout for the best if you like.

🏆 TWO-SIDED BRACKET
The classic bracket with the final in the middle. A gold line follows each winner all the way to the champion. Pinch to zoom or turn the phone to see it whole.

📝 RESULTS IN ONE TAP
"Pepe beat Luis 2–1", with an optional comment ("20 life to 0"). One game, best of 3 or best of 5.

♟️ PROPER STANDINGS
Points and tie-breakers like official Magic, Pokémon or Lorcana events. Works for any game: cards, ping-pong, FIFA, padel, chess…

📲 TAKE IT TO ANOTHER PHONE, NO ACCOUNTS
The whole tournament travels in a link or a QR code: scan it with the other phone's camera or send it in a chat. No servers.

📊 HALL OF FAME
Titles, finals and win rate of every player across all your tournaments. Export the bracket as an image or the results as CSV.

🚫 NO ADS, NO ACCOUNTS, NO PERMISSIONS
It asks for no permissions and no internet. Everything stays on your phone.
