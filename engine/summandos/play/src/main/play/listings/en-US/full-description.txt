Your squad of crossbowmen runs at a skeleton horde. On the way, gates with sums: +17, ×2, −9, ÷2. Take the one that leaves the most soldiers… and arrive with an army. ➕

Slide your finger to move between gates, and stand in front of each wave: you only hit what is ahead of you. Picking gates and aiming at once is the whole game.

• Four ranks, from easy sums to real maths:
  – Recruit: adding, taking away, ×2 and ÷2, with coloured gates.
  – Soldier: percentages and small sums such as ×(3+4).
  – Captain: powers, roots and equations, every gate gold, and 5 seconds to think through the hard ones.
  – Legend: 10 levels of two-step sums. Take the worse gate and you lose.
• 100 levels with up to 3 stars each.
• A giant boss every five levels: warrior, mage, Golem, rogue, Necromancer… They don't fall on contact: they eat soldiers until you empty their health bar. Dodge the fireballs.
• Weapon crates: pistols that double your damage and a bazooka that triples it, if you dare go for them.
• Pick your squad's colour; every colour stands apart from the enemies with colour blindness too.
• Sunset or night mode, upright or sideways.

No ads, no purchases, no account and no internet: nothing leaves your phone.
